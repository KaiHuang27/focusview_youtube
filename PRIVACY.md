# Privacy Policy

FocusView – Zoom, Rotate & Mirror for YouTube does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal data.

The extension only runs on YouTube pages to provide video zoom, rotation, and mirror controls inside the YouTube player.

This extension does not:

- collect personal information
- track user activity
- use cookies
- access browsing history
- access account information
- store personal user data
- send data to external servers
- use analytics or advertising trackers

The extension stores only a local zoom mode activation count, a reminder interval, a footer-click count capped at three, and whether a rating or support link has been clicked. Reminders appear every 20 activations by default. The first click on “Don’t remind me again” resets the count and changes reminders to every 60 activations; the second and subsequent clicks reset the count and change reminders to every 100 activations. Clicking either the rating or coffee link resets the count and restores the 10-activation interval, while retaining the footer-click count. Reminders permanently stop once the footer has been clicked at least three times and either external link has been clicked at least once, regardless of order. This only records a link click; no review or payment completion is checked. Closing with × or Escape keeps the current interval. These preferences contain no viewing history or personal information, are not synced, and are never transmitted. External rating and Ko-fi pages open only when you click their links.

All video transform controls are applied locally in the browser. No user data leaves the device.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer.
